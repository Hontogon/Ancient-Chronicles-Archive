  not necessarily de ja vu, but when you remember feeling something that you felt from something in the past after recently revisiting it:
example:

one day, you are talking to somebody about school, and you tell them that some of the assignments are too simple.
the person then asks you if you are in college "arent you in college?" (as if asking why then the assignments are too simple)
You reply with yes. 
A few days later, as you are working on something for school and find yourself thinking that it is an easy project. immediately your mind subconsiously recals the question that the person asked you back then when you told them about the simple assignments in the first place. 
"yes," you sort of think to yourself "aren't I supposed to be in college?"

At this point you have directly repeated the persons question or statement in your mind uncounsiously, agreeing with them and remembering that situation

Now a few years later....

Years have passed and all memory of that person have vanished. you dont remember those old conversations or anything. then one day, maybe you are reminded of that old coversation somehow.
a few days later, you are working on a project for school. you are still in college. you come across a simple project. you began to think how simple it is. then, without warning, that persons statement or question from years ago comes flooding back, and you remember not only that first affirmation, but every little affirmation that made you think of that first interaction way back then. mabye the first few times after the conversation where you found yourself repeating the person's question or statement: "yes, aren't I supposed to be in college?"

Now you have essentially connected two main points in time: the initial conversation, and the rememberance of right now years later, but youve also subconsiously connected all of those other little instances {usually closer to the initial conversation in the past} where you found yourself agreeing or restating that persons initial question or statement about college. 

note: however, if in the second instance of coming across a simple assignment you had not already reminded yourself of that converstation from years ago just a few days before, the new experience of doing that simple assignment probably would not have triggered that initial conversation memory at all because all memories and recalling of that persons question from years ago had erased. essentially, you needed a two step process to reconfirm that memory. 

step 1: somehow reminded of that person and several conversations you had (one of them being the one about college)

you make no correlation because the second rememberance hasnt happened yet, so no special attention is given to the college conversation memory at all more than any of the others.

step 2: the second encounter of a simple assignment occurs and now you actually remember the re-rememberance from a few days ago and that specific revisiting of the college  conversation in memory. now it means something

essentially, it is almost a two-factor authorization memory sequence used to trigger a pathway to a series of points in the past, with the main one being that initial conversation and others branching out of it like a web. those other points in the past are the very first times you encoutered an easy assignment in school and linked it to that person's conversation (which back then had only occured a few days or weeks ago).

This idea may essentially be used to form a sort of sequence for time travel or revisiting of past events in a memory or spirtual form. 

summary:  a two step process in the future unlocks a memory of the past which in turn creates a link from that point in the future to a cluster of different points within a certain time frame in the past, linked by one initial memory.

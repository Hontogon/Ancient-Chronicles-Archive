Hons Hontogon is a young [[royter.jpg]] who lives in [[Akaton Village]] in the modern day province of [[Akatonijia]]. 

---

appearance:

Hons bears the dark brown hair and eyes of most of the other members of his Akatonijian race. he usually wears the traditional Akatonian "cherry-orange" leather tunic and waistcoat, along with black cotton hose trousers and boots. Hons' defining trait is a bright red headband that he wears almost everywhere, also originating from his culture's tradition of amber to orange colored cotton headbands worn around the forehead

---

Name origin:

The name Hons is not too common in modern day Akatonijia, but is rather an ancient Akatonian root name  said to have originated from the name of a god

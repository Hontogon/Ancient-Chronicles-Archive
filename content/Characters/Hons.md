Hons is the supreme god of nature appointed by the [[Great Deity]] to be the leader of the nature gods and to watch over the nature ancients. 

Hons is also the first name of [[Hons Hontogon]], the young boy who helped restore the sacred power of time in the first chronicle: Destiny of Time, and who later was revealed to be the one bearing the title "the ancient hero"

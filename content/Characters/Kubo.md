
Kubo is the Ancient Wizard of Time appointed by the [[Great Deity]] and under the ordinance of the supreme god of time [[Askalon]] to watch over the [[six elements of time]], housed in the [[Shrine of Shrines]], and to protect the sacred power of time from the [[forces of evil]]. 

---

**Location**

Kubo resides in the [[Shrine of Shrines]] which is in the center of [[Akaton Village]], in the modern day province of [[Akatonijia]]

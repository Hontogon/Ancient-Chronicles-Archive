
The Guardians of WW are a group of protectors and enforcers of justice who watch over the lands across the world and have been around since the times of the [[Ancients]]

---

When the ancients traveled to Akaton, there was a group of protectors protecting them as they journeyed. This group eventually unofficially became the guardians of WW.

Things we know:

Guardians are like a group, but individualist at the same time

Sometimes help the ancients but not obligated to

Poses high knowledge of technology and advancements.

Became more official when [[Thompson]] arrived, but there are still a lot of unofficial ones.

It has been said before that the guardians were sent to [[Metropolitan]] in the past to help protect [[Metrok]]. In the process, their technological knowledge and advancements actually was the very thing that led to the modernization of Metrok.

The core idea here is that the guardians have pretty much been the backbone of the development and future of WW, bringing with them all the modern paths to the present day, and also inspiring those around them to do the same ( so the guardians themselves aren't Solely responsible for ALL of the advancements of WW, but they pretty much lead the race in advancement).

| 700  | <br>**The Ancients Arrive in Akaton:  <br><br>**The Ancients of Nature and their descendants, along with the one ancient of nature, arrive at the modern-day location of [[Akatonijia]]. There, they encounter people living in small settlements and groups, scattered across the Akaton Wilderness. The largest of these settlements is a small village called “Stream Town”, located south of the Akaton River. The Ancients decide to stay at this location for a while before resuming their journey.<br><br>**The naming of Akaton**<br><br>After arriving in modern day Akatonijia, the nature ancients settled in the land and named it "[[Akaton]]". The name is a conjugation of the names of the supreme gods of time and justice: [[Askalon]] and [[Hontogon]].<br><br>**Construction of Akaton Village**<br><br>The people of the land joined together, and under the guidance of the ancients and the Great Deity, they constructed the very first official establishment and future capital town of the entire province, [[Akaton Village]].<br><br>**Sacred Mountain:**<br><br>Sometime after the Ancients arrive in Akaton, one of the supreme gods, [[Hontgon]], speaks to [[Hogonon]], the leader of the Nature ancients, and summons him to a high mountain north of the wilderness. Hogonon along with the other three nature ancients head to the peak of the mountain. Once they reach the top, the Great Deity calls down fire to the mountain, and forms a cavern under the crest of the mountain. The Great Deity then pronounces the mountain to be the holy place where he will bestow power upon the nature ancients. He commands them to construct an inner sanctuary in the inner cavern for Him to bestow His power upon them. the mountain becomes known as [[Sacred Mountain]], and has retained that name until this day<br><br>**The Shrine of Shrines:**<br><br>In the year 10,000, one of the main events that took place during the golden age of Akaton was the construction of the [[Shrine of Shrines]]. During this time, the Great Deity instructed the Ancients of Nature and Time to work together to construct a sacred temple to house the [[six elements of time]] to protect the power of time from being abused by evil ones. The construction of the Shrine took seven days total. On the first day, The Great Deity sends the second world 90,000 years into the future to when it has been fully created. During these first six days, while the Shrine is built, The Great Deity sends the ancient of time [[Kubo]] to collect each of the six mirrored elements of time, three from their world (the first world), and three from the second world, Earth. On the seventh day, the shrine is completed, the second world (Earth) is rewound back in time to how it originally was, and a celebration is held in Akaton. The people of the land rejoice at the construction of the new sacred temple. The bell in the tower is rung 70 times throughout the entire day to celebrate the occasion.<br><br>**The Inner Sanctuary is Constructed:  <br>The Ancients build [[The Inner Sancturary]] inside the cavern of the mountain, according to the Great Deity’s specific instruction. The finished sanctuary consists of the Bridge of Spirits, the entryway, the interior containing the Pedestal of Nature, and the inner chamber. The Ancients then prepare to receive the power promised to them by the Great Deity.<br><br>**The Naming of Weird World:**<br><br>10,000 years after the creation of the worlds, the great Deity winds earth forward to the future, and takes Kubo there to gather the 6 elements on the 6 days the shrine of shrines is built. While there, Kubo learns the future language of English from earth and finds out about a man named Thompson coming to the first world in the future. He finds out that Thompson coins the name “weird world” for the new planet he discovers.<br><br>Kubo comes back to the first world with this info. He delivers the elements to the shrine, and tells the people that their planet (the first world) is to be called weird world. (in that time, it is phrased differently such as (strange planet) or (mysterious earth)). Up until now, the people of weird world didn’t need a reason to refer to their world by a specific name, because they thought their world was the only one in existence. Only the original ancients knew about the void second world. Once the first world is named, it is kept like that “strange planet or mysterious earth or whatever it was phrased in the language of that time”<br><br>**Time Script Created:**<br><br>With the knowledge of English, Kubo creates the language of time for the inscriptions on the shrine of shrines and other locations and artifacts related to the power of time<br><br>**The Pilgrimage of Nature:**<br><br>The Great Deity commands the nature ancients to conduct a holy pilgrimage under the guidance of four supreme gods: Hon, Jara, Nye, and Socrate.<br><br>**The First and Greatest Golden Age of Akaton:**<br><br>This very first era that spans across the entire 100 years of the year 700, from the nature ancients arriving in Akaton, to the time the Shrine of Shrines is created and beyond, is known as the very first and greatest golden age the land has ever seen.\| |

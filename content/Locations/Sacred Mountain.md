Sacred Mountain is the historical and modern day name of the tallest mountain that overlooks [[Akaton Village]] and the surrounding wilderness and backcountry.

The mountain is part of the Cayatan mountain range that circles around the north side of the Village and the overall valley of wilderness surrounding it. The mountain range is more broadly part of the main mass of mountains running through the center of the province of [[Akatonijia]] , from the border of [[Bijmontern]] to the west shores of the [[Analan Sea 1]]


When time began, there were many languages spread throughout the world. However, when the Great Diety appointed the 12 tribes of ancients to protect the three sacred powers, they each formed a special language based on the language used in the holy realm (the heavans, used by the gods (angels) and the Great Diety himself)

For the nature ancients specifically (the four ancients who protect akaton), their script was heavily based upon this heavently language. When they arrived in akaton and helped establish akaton village and the new province in the anceint days, their initial heavenly language based dialect became the official language of Akaton, spoken by all the people, complete with its own glyphs or letters.

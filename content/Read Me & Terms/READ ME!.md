
Welcome Readers!

It is truly my pleasure to have you.

My name is Michael and I am one of the creators of Ancient Chronicles.

I just want to begin by saying that this project didn't start by me sitting at a desk and coming up with the idea of this world out of nothing. It started when I was probably twelve years old, playing with my brother in the backyard or in the riverbed behind our house. But to us, it wasn't the backyard; it was the village square. The riverbed wasn't just a local creek, it was the vast, untamed Akaton Wilderness. And our hometown wasn't some small, pass-through type town in southern California; It was the ancient capital village of the province of Akatonijia, surrounded by vast, bamboo wilderness, flowing rivers, and towering mountains that had overlooked the land for thousands of years.

This is the Universe we created.

This is Ancient Chronicles~

Icon Guide -

Before you begin navigating through the archive, there are a few icons and guides I have created to help you fully understand the content within these pages...


First of all, you should understand that this archive holds not only in depth accounts of well established and fully thought out lore for the universe, I have also included tidbits and pages documenting works in project of the ever expanding lore of Ancient Chronicles. 

This means that you are likely to encounter ideas that may have not been fleshed out completely yet, and may be written in a more theoretical format, as if they are still being put together...

That's exactly the case!

You will find sections that I am currently developing, that I found necessary to add for those who would like to see my though process and because some of them can explain further upon the already well established lore contained in the archive, even if they aren't fully established themselves as solid ideas yet.

However, I am aware that not everyone likes to read through unfinished work and would rather only immerse themselves in the already well established elements of the Universe. 

I am also aware that many (if not most) of these WIP sections may contain spoilers, either major or minor to future novels, stories, or works within the Universe. In fact, I belive that even some finished sections may contain spoilers as well.

The Icon Guide -

For these reasons, I have created a simple icon guide that you can use to tell if a page is safe to read without encountering spoilers or if it is finished completely. Pages that contain major spoilers or are WIPS will be marked by a special symbol right under their title. Pages that are safe to read and are well established won't have any icon at all. So, all you have to do is check under the title of each page you navigate to to determine if it contains spoilers, is a WIP, or both.

Here is the guide:

☐ - This icon marks WIP pages that contain unfinished or unestablished lore/ideas
🛑 - This icon marks pages containing major spoilers

(please note that most WIP pages should be assumed to contain spoilers in general, and thus will not all be marked with the spoilers icon)

More on spoilers, this archive, & some background info on my creation process -

When I first started coming up with Ancient Chronicles, I developed a way of coming up with the different stories and lore with a very specific hierarchy in mind: I wanted people to be able to experience my stories through layers of revelation and finding out new things in a certain order to make it more fun and exciting. In one story you see a certain lore point, then as you read other stories, you start to connect the dots of the history of that lore and how it came to be in the universe. 

I really value that experience for my readers and partakers of this Worldbuilding journey, and so I want to give an important disclaimer regarding this archive:

Expect "Spoilers" on most pages

No, not every page will contain major story for future work, but by reading through this archive, you are getting to experience the lore through the eyes of the author and how I attempt to connect everything as it comes together and build that experience for my readers. Think of this as an invitation to join me along on the journey of creating this amazing universe.

In Conclusion, I am not saying there is a right or wrong way to experience the Universe of Ancient Chronicles, but rather depending on how you enjoy immersing in a universe, choose how to experience that immersion based on your own approach.

- If you would like to read only the absolute safe lore that everyone can know from the beginning, please exit this archive and enjoy reading the main lore and story information within the pages of the official Ancient Chronicles website:

https://hontogon.github.io/weird_world_webpage/index.html

- If you would like to join me for the exclusive journey of creating this world and universe as it is formed, then explore the pages of this amazing archive to your heart's content. Avoiding the spoiler icon pages will also ensure that you can still feel the excitement and discovery of a lot of new lore and ideas as you immerse yourself in future stories or content. (note: some pages will contain minor spoilers even if the page is not marked as stated before. I will do my best to add the spoiler icon inside the page right before the paragraph or section containing said minor spoilers for those wanting to skip those parts)

- Finally, If you would like to fully engage with the Ancient Chronicles Universe and join in on the process of seeing that lore come together and how the stories unfold before they are even released, then by all means, feel free to read through the spoiler and WIP pages and discover the amazing connections of this world along with me.


The Journey is yours to choose,
